## Category Templates

### 1. INBOUND LEADS / CLIENT ACQUISITION
**Goal**: Convert readers into paying clients

**Best Structures**:
- Problem-Agitation-Solution (make pain real, then solve)
- Assumed Close (ask which, not whether)
- Social Proof + Numbers (show you've done it)
- Objection Handling (counter objections)
- Framework Reveal (give away valuable method)

**Key Elements**:
- Strong problem statement (what's the pain?)
- Social proof (I've helped X people do this)
- Specific results/numbers
- Easy-to-say-yes CTA (book call, DM for details, not "buy now")
- Urgency signals (limited spots, time-sensitive)

**Tone Examples**:
- "I helped 50+ founders in your exact situation... here's what they all did wrong"
- "Stop leaving money on the table. Here's exactly how to fix it."
- "The #1 mistake I see... and how to fix it"

**Power Words**: Transform, leverage, scale, multiply, dominate, penetrate

## Writing Process

### Step 1: Understand the Context
- What's the goal? (clients, audience, both)
- What's the category? (leads, audience, authority, personal)
- What's the topic/keyword?
- Who's the target audience?
- What tone is desired?

### Step 2: Choose Structure
- Pick template based on goal and category
- Pick creator DNA (tone/style)
- Identify hook type (problem, contrarian, story, stat)

### Step 3: Write the Hook (First Sentence)
- Must stop the scroll
- Should relate to pain point or curiosity
- Keep to 1-2 sentences
- Make people want to read next sentence

### Step 4: Build the Body
- Follow chosen structure
- Use short paragraphs (scannable)
- Use line breaks liberally
- Include proof/evidence
- Make each sentence earn its place
- Remove filler words

### Step 5: End with CTA
- Must align with goal
- Should be easy to say yes to
- Include action verb
- Create urgency if appropriate

### Step 6: Final Polish
- Check for authenticity (remove corporate speak)
- Check for length (target: 150-300 words for most)
- Ensure emotional resonance
- Verify proof/credibility
- Confirm alignment with user's goal

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Too generic**: "I'm excited to share..." / "Let me tell you a story..."
❌ **Too salesy**: "Click here NOW!" / "Limited time only!!!"
❌ **Unclear hook**: Start rambling instead of hooking first
❌ **No proof**: Making claims without evidence
❌ **Wrong tone**: Aggressive when should be vulnerable, vice versa
❌ **Weak CTA**: "Let me know what you think" (doesn't drive action)
❌ **Too long**: More than 400 words (loses attention)
❌ **No structure**: Feels like rambling
❌ **Inauthentic**: Sounds like AI (use contractions, casual language)
❌ **Wrong category**: Using audience-building hook for client acquisition
